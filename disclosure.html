Affiliate disclosure

How Window Safar earns

Window Safar is free to ride. To keep it running, some links on the site are affiliate links. If you click one and then book or buy something, Window Safar may earn a small commission. You pay exactly the same price as you would going to the site directly.

Which links

  • Book this train links to train booking sites such as ixigo, sometimes through affiliate networks like Cuelinks or EarnKaro.
  • Stay in… links to hotel listings on Booking.com, through the Awin affiliate network.
  • Take home… links to products on Amazon.in.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

What isn't paid

Routes, food, station stories and songs are chosen because they belong on the journey, not because anyone paid for them. No brand has paid to appear on Window Safar. If that ever changes, sponsored content will be clearly labelled as sponsored.

Tips

"Buy the maker a chai" is a voluntary tip by UPI. It doesn't unlock anything, and it goes straight to me.

The Pass

The ₹149 Window Safar Pass shown on the site is a demo for now, and no payment is taken.
