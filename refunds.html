Refunds and cancellations

The Window Safar Pass costs ₹149 for one year. If it isn't right for you, here's how getting your money back works.

7-day refund, no questions asked

If you're not happy with the Pass, ask for a refund within 7 days of buying it and you'll get the full ₹149 back. You don't need to give a reason. Once the refund is processed, the Pass is removed from your account.

Always refunded

  • If you were charged twice for the same Pass.
  • If you paid but the Pass didn't unlock and I can't fix it within 2 working days.

After 7 days

After the first 7 days, the Pass isn't refundable, but it stays active on your account until the full year is over. If a Pass route stops working for a long time, I'll replace it with another route or refund you for the time left.

Cancelling

There's nothing to cancel. The Pass is a one-time payment and does not renew automatically, so you won't be charged again unless you choose to buy it again. You can also delete your account any time from the sign-in page.

How to ask for a refund

Email prachidec03@gmail.com from the email you signed in with, or use the contact page. Include the date you paid and, if you have it, the payment ID from your receipt.

How long it takes

I'll reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the same card, UPI or bank account you paid with, and usually reach you within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
